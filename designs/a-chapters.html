It started on a wall.

To do, Doing, Done in marker, and a sticky note for every task. Asanaka keeps that wall and adds what paper can’t: who has the task, when it’s due, and the same board on every screen in the team.

Make a card yourself

Name it.

One line that says what has to happen.

Give it to someone.

Their initials sit on the card, so nobody asks whose it is.

Say when it’s due.

Today turns yellow. Late turns red before anyone has to say so.

Say how much it matters.

High sits on a red block, so it is the first thing you see in a busy column.

Move it along.

The field behind it turns the colour of its section. Put it in Done and it finishes itself.
