Asa na ka?The board knows.

Cebuano for “where are you now?” Asanaka is a kanban board for a small team. Drag a card and everyone sees where it went.

Sign in

A live board. Drag a card, or tap its dot.

Every task has an address.

Its section is its street: To do, Doing, Review, Done. One look at the board says where a task stands, the way a pin on a map says where you are.

Cebu City in 1699, on a museum wall. The language in our name is spoken here. Photo: Agravanteflav, CC0, Wikimedia Commons.

Open the board How it’s built

How a card moves

First, write it down.

Click Add task at the bottom of a section, type, press Enter. The box stays open for the next one.

Then drag it to where it is.

Pick the card up and the others make room. Drop it in Doing and everyone sees it there.

Tap the dot when it’s done.

The dot fills, the card glides into Done, and the column counts itself again.
