Asa na ka?

Cebuano for “where are you now?” Asanaka is a kanban board that answers it for every task your team has.

Sign in

Every jeepney says where it goes.

7B runs Apas, Lahug, Jones, Carbon. The stops are painted down the side in order, so you know the route before you board. A task on Asanaka carries its route the same way.

To doDoingReviewDone

Departures: every unfinished task, by when it’s due. Press a status to move the task along.

    First you add it.

    Type a name at the bottom of a section and press Enter. The box stays open for the next one.

    Then you drag it along.

    Pick it up and the cards around it make room. Drop it where it is now.

    Someone checks it over.

    In Review, the person who checks it sees every comment and subtask on it.

    It lands in Done.

    Tick it, and the board says so out loud before the card settles in Done.

    Pick a card up and the others make room. Drop it in another section and the whole team sees the move.

    Drag itanywhere.

    Click a card and its pane slides in: who has it, when it’s due, its subtasks and comments. Escape puts you back where you were.

    Open thewhole task.

    Tick a task and the board says Nahuman na, Cebuano for “it’s finished,” across the bottom of the screen. Then the card settles in Done.
